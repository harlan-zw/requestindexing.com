When a page is missing from Google, this is how I read the record.

Open Indexing and pick your Site. You land on Overview, under Indexing in the sidebar.

{INDEXING_URL}

The card called Why pages stop follows your inspected URLs through four stages: Inspected, Crawled, Indexable, Indexed. Start at the biggest drop. The card names it in one line.

Here is the fix I try first for each reason:

- Google does not know the URL. Add it to your sitemap. Link to it from a page Google already indexed.
- Google knows the URL but has not crawled it. Link to it from your indexed pages. Cut thin URLs, such as empty tag pages.
- Google could not fetch the URL. Check robots.txt, server errors, logins, and redirects.
- A signal tells Google to skip the URL. Look for a noindex tag. Make sure the canonical points to the page itself.
- Google crawled the page and chose not to index it. Compare it with similar pages on your Site. Improve it, or merge it into the stronger page.

Under Indexing, URLs shows the reason Google gives for each URL. Recovery groups the pages Google refuses.

After a fix, the URLs page shows the latest URL Inspection verdict for that URL. Google publishes no time for a recrawl. Asking again does not make it faster.

If a reason makes no sense, reply with the URL. I'll look at it with you.
