I'm Harlan. I maintain Nuxt SEO, the SEO modules for Nuxt. I built Request Indexing to answer one question: is my page indexed, and if not, why?

You publish a page, then you wait for Google to show it. Most indexing tools stop at the submit button. They send Google a notification. After that, you see nothing.

I wanted the record: what I submitted, what Google decided, and what changed since last week. Request Indexing keeps that record for you. It checks the URLs in your sitemaps with URL Inspection. If Google does not index a page, you see the reason Google gives.

One thing to do now: open Indexing and pick your Site.
